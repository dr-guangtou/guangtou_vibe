# arXiv API Terms of Use (Summary)

## Rate Limits and Connection Rules

- Rate limits apply across all machines under your control.
- When using legacy APIs (including OAI-PMH, RSS, and the arXiv API), make no more than one request every three seconds.
- Limit requests to a single connection at a time.
- Do not attempt to bypass, parallelize, or otherwise circumvent the rate limits.
- If higher rates are needed, contact arXiv support instead of increasing request frequency.

## Acceptable Use

- Use the APIs for scholarly and research purposes consistent with arXiv's mission.
- Metadata is available under CC0; e-print contents remain under the authors' licenses.
- Do not redistribute full-text content unless the license permits it or you have permission.

## Operational Expectations

- Do not represent your project as endorsed by arXiv without permission.
- Identify yourself in a way that allows arXiv to contact you if your usage causes issues.
